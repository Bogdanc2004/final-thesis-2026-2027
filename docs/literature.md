Namaa, Javaan Chahl<br>*DOI:* [10.3390/automation5020007](https://doi.org/10.3390/automation5020007) | **Hardware:** Raspberry Pi 4 Model B + OV5647 camera module (compatible with RPi 2/3/4, up to 5 MP, 2592 × 1944 px, JPG), mounted 1–1.5 m from the soil at a 45° angle.<br><br>**ML Model:** Random Forest (RF).<br><br>**Method:**<br>• **Dataset:** ~900 images collected over 3 months across 3 weather conditions (*sunny, cloudy, dusty*), 2 soil types (*sandy soil, peat moss soil*), and 2 states (*dry, wet*).<br>• **Preprocessing:** Selected the Region of Interest (ROI), applied the Hough Circle algorithm to detect the soil, used the center of the rectangle as the reference point, and cropped to a fixed border of 227 × 180 px.<br>• **Feature Extraction:** Split images into 6 color channels for RF training — **RGB** ($R, G, B$) and **YCbCr** ($Y, Cb, Cr$) — as this combination better captures differences between soil states than RGB alone.<br>• **Data Split:** 90% training / 10% validation.<br>• **Actuation:** Water pumps are triggered only when the RF classifier predicts the soil is *dry*. | **Evaluation Metrics:**<br>• Confusion Matrix<br>• $\text{Accuracy} = \frac{TP + TN}{TP + TN + FP + FN}$<br>• $\text{Precision} = \frac{TP}{TP + FP}$<br>• $\text{Recall} = \frac{TP}{TP + FN}$<br>• $\text{F1-Score} = \frac{2 \times \text{Precision} \times \text{Recall}}{\text{Precision} + \text{Recall}}$<br>• $\text{MCC} = \frac{TP \times TN -
